# Project 3: 3-Year Corporate Financial Statement Analysis & Performance Dashboard
### **Tool Stack:** MS Excel (Advanced Formulas) | Financial Modeling & Ratio Analytics
### **Data Bounds:** 3-Year Historical Tracking (FY2024 - FY2026) 
### **Core Analysis Dimensions:** Horizontal Trend Analysis, Vertical Margin Drags, Liquidity Constraints

---

## 📌 Project Executive Summary
This project delivers a deep-dive corporate financial analysis of a multi-year enterprise operating pipeline. Using a 3-year historical ledger matrix, the study builds formulaic frameworks to evaluate top-line revenue trajectories, structural cost inflation drags, short-term debt servicing capacities, and core asset efficiency vulnerabilities. 

The final output bridges the gap between raw ledger rows and strategic corporate decision-making by exposing hidden operational traps (such as stagnant working capital layers) and outlining concrete, board-ready turnaround recommendations.

---

## 🛠️ Financial Model Architecture & Analytical Framework
The project's analytical logic is built entirely on dynamic double-entry tracking and corporate finance formulas:
* **Horizontal Analysis Matrix:** Evaluates year-over-year (YoY) percentage growth rates across revenue, direct variable production inputs, and net earnings.
* **Vertical Analysis Layer:** Normalizes the company's income profile by expressing every expense and profit margin component as a direct percentage of Gross Revenue.
* **Liquidity & Leverage Engine:** Computes structural solvency positions using the Current Ratio (Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities) and maps debt exposure against current assets.

## 🔍 Major Structural Discoveries & Audit Insights

### 1. The Cost Squeeze Drag (Profitability Analysis)
Between FY24 and FY25, revenue remained entirely flat. However, production costs expanded by **2.45%**, forcing the Gross Margin down from **41.22% to 39.77%**. In FY26, a massive ₹1.04 Lakh top-line volume breakout occurred, but absolute Net Profit remained frozen at **₹81,309** because input expenses surged symmetrically by **9.61%**. This proves the enterprise is experiencing intense input cost inflation and lacks pricing power to pass costs to customers.

### 2. The Liquid Cushion Deficit (Working Capital Analysis)
The company’s Current Ratio is structurally locked at an alarming **1.10x** across all 36 months. With Current Assets at ₹4,99,270 and Current Liabilities at ₹4,53,785, the company operates with a razor-thin short-term cash margin of just ₹45,485. 

### 3. The Accounting Static Red Flag
An inspection reveals that **Current Assets, Current Liabilities, and Total Debt are identical down to the single rupee** across FY24, FY25, and FY26. In a live corporate environment, this indicates highly illiquid asset management, potentially signaling completely frozen customer collections (Accounts Receivable) or massive amounts of dead, unmoving inventory trapped on warehouse shelves.

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations Executed in Model
1. **Procurement Value-Engineering:** Immediate vendor re-negotiations are mandatory to compress the Cost of Sales back below the 60% threshold to recover historic profit margins.
2. **Asset Liquidation Drills:** Initiate an internal audit to flush out slow-moving inventory and liquidate it for immediate cash inflow to expand the working capital safety margin.
3. **Debt Re-Tenuring:** Convert a portion of the ₹3,47,530 debt into long-term structures to immediately reduce current liabilities and pull the Current Ratio up to a healthy **1.50x–1.75x**.
