## 1. Project Overview
This project simulates an end-to-end statutory audit engagement for a fictional client, Green Leaf Manufacturing Ltd (a UK-based consumer goods manufacturing SME with roughly £10m in annual revenue). 

The goal of the project was to design a review-ready audit file that mirrors the logic, thresholds, and documentation standards expected of a first-year audit junior at a major firm. The file systematically moves from initial planning and materiality calculations to risk mapping, sample substantive testing, and a final conclusion.

## 2. Methodology & Key Deliverables

### Planning & Materiality Framework
* **Benchmark Selected:** Total Revenue (£10,000,000) was chosen as the most relevant benchmark for a stable manufacturing business of this scale.
* **Planning Materiality:** Set at 1% of the revenue benchmark, establishing a threshold of **£100,000**.
* **Performance Materiality:** Set at 75% of planning materiality (**£75,000**) to serve as a practical safety buffer against aggregated uncorrected misstatements.

### Risk Mapping & Control Responses
An initial risk matrix was built to map out testing priorities across key balance sheet and profit & loss line items:
* **Revenue Recognition:** Evaluated as **High Risk** due to inherent management incentives to hit growth goals. Response: Substantive transaction sampling.
* **Inventory Valuation:** Evaluated as **High Risk** due to complex costings and potential obsolescence of physical consumer products. Response: Valuation testing.
* **Cash & Purchases:** Evaluated as **Medium Risk** for processing errors or year-end cut-off issues. Response: Full bank reconciliation and late-period cut-off sweeps.

### Substantive Testing Log & Exception Tracking
A sample substantive testing sheet was executed to test explicit financial statement assertions (Existence, Accuracy, and Completeness). 
* **Key Finding / Exception:** During revenue testing, transaction `INV-102` (£1,440) was flagged for a missing receipt. 
* **Audit Impact Evaluation:** Because this single exception totals £1,440—which falls significantly below our Performance Materiality threshold of £75,000—it does not indicate a systemic or material threat to the accounts. 

## 3. Core Findings & Audit Conclusion
Based on the testing protocols completed and the evidence logged in the workbook:
1. The financial statements of Green Leaf Manufacturing Ltd for the year ended 31 March 2025 are considered fairly stated and free from material misstatement.
2. An **unmodified (clean) audit opinion** would be issued to the stakeholders based on this file.
3. **Internal Control Recommendation:** While the logged exception is financially immaterial, management should strengthen document-retention controls around revenue processing to eliminate administrative gaps in future cycles.
