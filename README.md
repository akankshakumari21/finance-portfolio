# Corporate Finance, Audit, and Tax Analysis Portfolio

A structured collection of practical accounting, statutory audit, and tax computation projects. This repository demonstrates end-to-end expertise in modern accounting workflows, corporate tax compliance, and financial reporting standards.

## 🛠️ Skills & Tools Featured
*   **Accounting Frameworks:** Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS), Bookkeeping Lifecycle.
*   **Software & Platforms:** Zoho Books / Zoho Ecosystem, Microsoft Excel [or Google Sheets].
*   **Core Competencies:** Statutory Auditing, Tax Planning & Computation, Internal Controls, Documentation.

---

## 📂 Repository Structure & Project Breakdown

### 📁 [01-Audit Working Papers](./01-audit-working-papers)
Standardised audit document templates and working files designed to satisfy compliance and statutory review requirements.
*   **Key Focus:** Audit trial balance mapping, internal control verification, substantive testing workflows, and analytical review procedures.
*   **Impact:** Ensures thorough audit evidence collection and transparent documentation for executive review.

### 📁 [02-Indian Bookkeeping - Zoho](./02-indian-bookkeeping-zoho)
Cloud-based bookkeeping architecture configured to align with Indian accounting principles and commercial frameworks.
*   **Key Focus:** Chart of accounts design, journal entries, accounts payable/receivable automation, and bank reconciliation using Zoho Books.
*   **Impact:** Simplifies data ingestion pipelines and streamlines real-time financial reporting for small-to-medium enterprises.

### 📁 [03-Tax Computation](./03-tax-computation)
Automated tax calculation schedules and analytical templates designed to forecast corporate tax liabilities accurately.
*   **Key Focus:** Income tax computations, deduction schedules, deferred tax evaluations, and provisions matching relevant local financial year guidelines.
*   **Impact:** Minimizes calculation variance errors and enforces strict compliance with statutory corporate tax structures.
